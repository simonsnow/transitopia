# Boundary Route 1 (BC Transit)

Boundary Regional Transit Route 1 runs between Greenwood and Grand Forks on Fridays and requires advance booking. Its four scheduled trips are transcribed from BC Transit's published schedule; on-request Routes 2 and 70 are not included. It is intentionally line-only (`routes.json` → `vehicles: false`) so the map does not imply a bus runs without a booking.

The shape follows OpenStreetMap roads, with approximate community-centre endpoints. BC Transit terms: https://www.bctransit.com/open-data/terms-of-use/ (identify BC Transit as the source; do not use its marks).

What's still assumed is in [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md); when one is answered, update the config and move the answer here with its source.
